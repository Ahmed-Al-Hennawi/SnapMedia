Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Overview

SnapMedia aims to work without user accounts. This draft describes the included software, not yet the policies of a future hosting company or advertising provider. Review and update it before public launch.

2. Data we handle

When you submit a URL, the server temporarily processes that URL, technical media metadata, your requested format and quality, and a randomly generated job identifier. A hosting service may also process IP addresses and standard access/security logs. We do not request your social-network passwords.

3. Temporary downloads

Generated media files are held on the download server and normally deleted within about one hour via a periodic cleanup process. Anyone with the unguessable temporary download link may access that file while it exists, so do not share it casually.

4. Local preferences and outside services

The browser may save your chosen language in local storage. The current design loads Google Fonts, which can involve network requests to Google. Media thumbnails may load directly from their source platform. The server contacts the platform you linked to retrieve eligible content.

5. Advertising and cookies

The current website does not load paid advertising scripts. If advertising is added later, this policy must be updated and any required consent controls put in place before activation.

6. Security and retention

Downloads use random identifiers, HTTPS media URLs are validated against a small set of supported platform domains, and temporary files are removed periodically. Hosting logs, backups, analytics or other services may have separate retention periods, which the operator must disclose before launch.

7. Your rights and contact

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to some processing. Contact ahmed.alhennawi9@gmail.com with privacy requests. This document is a starter template and should be reviewed by a qualified professional before public launch.

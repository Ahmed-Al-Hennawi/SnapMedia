Copyright Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SnapMedia is a temporary media-processing service. It does not host a public library of user submissions. Submitting a public URL does not establish a right to copy the media. Users must own the content, have permission from the relevant rights holders, or have another applicable legal basis, and must comply with platform rules.

Report an infringement concern

Email ahmed.alhennawi9@gmail.com with “Copyright report” in the subject. Please include:

  • Your name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • A description of the work you believe you own or represent, and the basis for your authority.
  • The SnapMedia page, temporary download URL, or source URL involved, if known.
  • What use concerns you and why you believe it is unauthorised.
  • Any supporting evidence that helps identify the work and alleged use.

Do not send account passwords or unnecessary personal data. We may ask for clarification or evidence before acting.

How reports are handled

The operator will review the information available and, where a relevant temporary file is still present, may remove it or restrict access where technically possible. Files normally expire after about one hour, so a report may arrive after the file has already been deleted. The operator may preserve limited information where needed to respond to a legal claim. A rights holder may also need to report the original content to the source platform.

Repeat misuse and incorrect reports

SnapMedia does not currently have user accounts, so there is no account-strike system. Where technically possible, repeated abusive activity may be blocked or rate-limited. Please submit reports in good faith; knowingly false or misleading claims can harm others and may carry legal consequences.

UK and international requests

This process is intended to make copyright concerns easy to report. It is not a claim that SnapMedia is registered as a US DMCA agent or that any particular safe-harbour regime applies. If formal legal notices are required, obtain advice for the applicable jurisdiction and hosting arrangement.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SnapMedia currently uses browser storage to remember interface choices. Its own code does not currently load analytics or advertising scripts. Third-party services and a future host may have separate practices, which the operator must verify and disclose before launch. See the Privacy Policy for the wider data flow.

Storage used by this site

  • Language preference: the snapmedia_lang local-storage value remembers the language you select. It remains until you change it or clear this site's browser data.
  • Privacy preferences: when you make a choice in the privacy controls, a local-storage value remembers it on this browser until you change it or clear site data. This preference does not activate advertising or analytics.

Advertising and analytics

No AdSense or analytics script is active in this version. No advertising or analytics cookies are intentionally set by SnapMedia's own code. If either category is introduced later, this policy and the consent controls must be updated to describe the actual providers, purposes and duration, and visitors must be asked again before non-essential technology is loaded where consent is required. A choice made now is not consent to unspecified future advertising.

Third-party requests

The site loads Google Fonts from Google servers. A video thumbnail may load from its source platform after you submit a link. Those requests may expose your IP address and browser details to the receiving service. If you follow the Buy Me a Coffee link, that service's privacy and cookie practices apply on its site. SnapMedia cannot confirm a future hosting provider's own cookies or access-log retention until that provider is chosen.

Change your choices

Use the “Privacy choices” control in the site footer to review or change optional preferences. You can also clear this site's browser data in your browser settings. Essential or requested interface storage may continue to be used without optional tracking. Rejecting optional categories does not affect downloads.

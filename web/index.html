GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is SnapMedia free?+

Yes. SnapMedia is free and needs no account or subscription. Coffee support is entirely optional.

Can I download private videos?+

No. SnapMedia is for accessible public media you own or have permission to download. We don't bypass protected content.

What is the difference between standard and HD?+

Standard video targets up to 720p. HD targets up to 1080p, when available. MP3 offers 128 or 192 kbps output; neither option improves the original recording.

Do you keep my videos?+

Generated downloads are stored temporarily on the server and automatically expire after about one hour. Check our Privacy Policy for more information.
